# Data Sources and Access Audit

**Status:** Phase 0 candidate inventory; no dataset has yet been accepted into a pilot bundle
**Verified:** 2026-08-21
**Local repository data:** none

## 1. Acceptance policy

A web page being visible is not sufficient evidence that a dataset is fit for hydraulic modelling. Before use, each source must pass:

1. authoritative source and chain-of-custody review;
2. licence/terms review, including attribution and redistribution;
3. checksum and acquisition timestamp capture;
4. CRS, axis order, vertical datum, units, timestamp semantics, nodata, and resolution inspection;
5. geometry/raster validity, completeness, duplicate, and plausible-range checks;
6. comparison of metadata claims with actual fields/content;
7. pilot-area coverage check;
8. scientific fitness review for the intended use;
9. quality/provenance classification shown to users.

A derived or third-party mirror is not silently treated as the primary authority. Raw downloads remain immutable, and a manifest records URL, licence, checksum, access time, source version, and transformations.

## 2. Data status vocabulary

| Machine status | Dashboard label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `OBSERVED_REALTIME` | REAL DATA | A current observation/estimate, with observation time and latency shown |
| `OBSERVED_HISTORICAL` | HISTORICAL DATA | Past observation or remotely sensed estimate |
| `EXTERNAL_FORECAST` | EXTERNAL FORECAST | Forecast generated by an identified external model/provider |
| `SIMULATED_SCENARIO` | SIMULATED DATA | Deterministic synthetic/demo forcing or assumed infrastructure scenario |
| `STATIC_REFERENCE` | REAL STATIC DATA or REFERENCE DATA | Real-world static mapping such as DEM/roads; age and limitations shown |
| `MODEL_PREDICTION` | MODEL PREDICTION | UFNS simulation/forecast output, never an observation |
| `DERIVED` | DERIVED DATA | Computed from source data; parent lineage shown |

“Real” does not mean “accurate enough for this use.” Quality flags and native resolution remain visible.

### Rainfall source decision

- **Guaranteed demo path:** deterministic, seeded scenario rainfall with explicit units and labels.
- **Guaranteed baseline:** persistence forecast operating on the chosen current/last rainfall field.
- **Historical evaluation path:** IMERG/GSMaP-ISRO/IMD as appropriate to the metric scale; no dataset has yet been downloaded.
- **Live path:** provider adapters are optional and fail visibly. IMD/MOSDAC access should be requested in parallel by the human team; unavailable credentials must not be stored in Git.
- **Unknown:** no confirmed open Indian radar rain-rate API currently satisfies quantitative, high-resolution, low-latency, licenced programmatic access.

**Rule:** the pilot defaults to 30 m if a global DSM is the best accepted source. Resampling it to 10 m is a rendering/alignment operation and remains flagged `RESAMPLED`; it does not authorize 10 m scientific claims.

## 6. Drainage and water infrastructure

This is the highest-risk data category.

| Source | Content | Access / licence | Candidate use | Critical caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Bhuvan Store urban products](https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/2dresources/bhuvanstore2.php) | NUIS urban land use (1:10,000) and AMRUT urban data (listed at 1:4,000 for 232 towns), WMS/WMTS | Official ISRO/NRSC web services; service terms and layer schema require review | Primary-source discovery and comparison | A map service layer is not necessarily downloadable or hydraulically complete. Layer identifiers, coverage, terms, and attributes must be audited. |
| [India Geodata urban water collection](https://github.com/yashveeeeeeer/india-geodata/tree/main/data/water/urban-water) | SBM stormwater drains; West Bengal AMRUT drain lines and stormwater vent points; Parquet/PMTiles/compressed GeoJSONL | Aggregator metadata states India Open Government Licence and cites MoHUA/AMRUT/SBM; approximately 50 MB | Fast candidate for pilot data audit, especially a small West Bengal town | Secondary 2026 aggregator. Primary provenance, actual attributes, geometry, coverage, and licence must be independently verified. Presence of line/point geometry does not imply diameters, inverts, connectivity, capacity, pumps, or outlets. |
| [Bengaluru Stormwater Drains Maps on OpenCity](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bengaluru-stormwater-drains-maps) | 2022 KML for primary, secondary, tertiary and combined stormwater drains | Public download; dataset-specific licence/primary authority must be checked | Alternate pilot plan geometry | Published map linework may omit dimensions/inverts/inlets/outlets and may not be a connected pipe/channel graph. |
| [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) via Overpass | `waterway=drain`, ditches, culverts, waterways, occasional manholes/inlets | ODbL 1.0 with attribution/share-alike database obligations; public Overpass fair-use limits | Supplement and topology comparison | Completeness is highly variable and underground systems are commonly missing. Not a capacity model. Freeze extracts for reproducibility. |
| Human-supplied SWMM `.inp` / municipal asset GIS | Nodes, links, subcatchments, dimensions, inverts, pumps/outfalls | Not available | Best path to real drainage hydraulics | Must validate CRS/vertical datum, units, connectivity, calibration, licence, and sensitive-infrastructure constraints. |
| Deterministic synthetic network fixture | Fully parameterized inlets, junctions, conduits and outlet | Generated and committed by UFNS with a documented seed/design | Solver verification and guaranteed four-scenario demo | Always `SIMULATED_SCENARIO`; never display as the actual city network. Real terrain/roads used around it keep their separate statuses. |

### Drainage audit requirements

A candidate network must report:

- unique node/link counts and disconnected components;
- line endpoint snapping tolerance and changes made;
- flow direction evidence;
- ground and invert elevation source/vertical datum;
- conduit shape/dimensions, material/roughness, inlet capture, outlet and pump data coverage;
- invalid/duplicate/crossing geometry;
- consistency with terrain and mapped receiving waters;
- percentage of parameters that are measured, published, derived, or assumed.

If any hydraulic field is absent, the model may use an explicit scenario assumption but the network/run is not called a representation of actual capacity.

| Source | Content | Proposed use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|

No flood model metric will be reported until an independent, aligned reference is actually ingested. A literature figure or a model output from another paper is not automatically reusable ground truth.

## 9. Pilot recommendation and unresolved selection

### Preferred candidate: data-audited West Bengal AMRUT urban subset

Reason: candidate drain lines **and vent points** exist in the identified open collection, making surface/drain exchange more demonstrable than linework alone. Select only one approximately 4 km × 4 km catchment after inspecting actual coverage and attributes. The collection name does not prove that central Kolkata is covered.

### Fallback: Bengaluru subset

Reason: public primary/secondary/tertiary stormwater drain KML and strong OSM road coverage. Limitations include unknown hydraulic attributes and likely absent inlet/invert information.

### Guaranteed reproducible scientific fixture

A small synthetic urban catchment and fully parameterized SWMM network will accompany any real-area pilot for solver verification and blockage comparison. The UI will label it simulated. This avoids making demo reproducibility depend on external APIs while not disguising synthetic infrastructure as observed.

### Human/data-owner actions

1. Approve the city/pilot selection criteria.
2. Request IMD API and suitable MOSDAC data access early.
3. Seek municipal/academic access to surveyed DEM/LiDAR, SWMM/CAD drainage, inlet/invert/capacity, pump/outfall operation, and timestamped flood observations.
4. Confirm whether a non-commercial/share-alike DEM such as FABDEM is acceptable to the team's intended distribution.
5. Identify an emergency-management reviewer for routing/passability thresholds.
